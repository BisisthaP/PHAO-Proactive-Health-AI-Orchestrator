# NG28 — Type 2 diabetes in adults: management (Summary)

> **Note:** This is a condensed, educational summary of NICE guideline NG28 for use by
> the PHAO demo system. It is not a substitute for the full official guideline. Always
> confirm against the current NICE publication before any clinical decision.

## HbA1c targets

- For adults managed by lifestyle and diet, or by a single drug not associated with
  hypoglycaemia, support a target HbA1c of **48 mmol/mol (6.5%)**.
- For adults on a drug associated with hypoglycaemia (for example a sulfonylurea), support
  a target HbA1c of **53 mmol/mol (7.0%)**.
- Consider relaxing the target on a case-by-case basis for people who are frail or have a
  reduced life expectancy.

Measure HbA1c at 3–6 month intervals (tailored to individual needs) until HbA1c is stable
on unchanging therapy, then at 6-month intervals.

## First-line drug treatment

- Offer standard-release **metformin** as the initial drug treatment, titrating the dose
  gradually to minimise gastrointestinal side effects.
- If metformin is contraindicated or not tolerated, consider a modified-release
  preparation or an alternative agent.

## Cardiovascular risk and SGLT2 inhibitors

For adults with type 2 diabetes who have chronic heart failure, established
atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, or who are at high risk of cardiovascular disease,
offer an **SGLT2 inhibitor** with proven cardiovascular benefit in addition to metformin.

## Lifestyle and self-management

Provide structured education, dietary advice and support for physical activity, weight
management and smoking cessation. Encourage self-monitoring where appropriate.

## Monitoring for complications

Arrange annual review including HbA1c, blood pressure, renal function (eGFR and
urinary albumin:creatinine ratio), lipid profile, retinal screening and foot assessment.
